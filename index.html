Shipping Info

We ship across South Africa. Orders are typically processed within 24-48 hours and delivered within 2-5 business days depending on your location. Delivery fees are calculated at checkout via WhatsApp based on your address. Major metro areas (Johannesburg, Cape Town, Durban, Pretoria) usually see faster turnaround.

Once your order ships, we'll send you tracking details directly via WhatsApp so you always know where your package is.

Exchange Policy

Not the right fit? You can exchange unworn items in original packaging within 7 days of delivery for a different size or item of equal value.

To start an exchange, message us on WhatsApp with your order details and reason for the exchange. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in original condition with tags attached. Sale items are final unless faulty.

Privacy Policy

We only collect the information needed to process your order — your name, contact number, and delivery address, shared with us directly via WhatsApp.

We never sell or share your personal information with third parties. Your details are used solely to fulfill and communicate about your order with FORBIDDEN STORE.
